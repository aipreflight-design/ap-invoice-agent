# Supply Agreement – TechParts GmbH

Between: Klant NV ("the Customer") and TechParts GmbH, VAT DE811234567 ("the Supplier").
Effective date: 15 June 2024.

## Section 1 – Scope
The Supplier delivers spare parts for conveyor systems as ordered by the Customer through purchase orders.

## Section 2 – Prices
Prices are as stated on each purchase order and are firm for that order.

## Section 3 – Quality and warranty
All parts carry a 12-month warranty from delivery. In case of repeated quality defects, the Customer may suspend the Supplier. During a suspension, the Customer shall not pay any invoices of the Supplier until the quality incident has been formally closed by the Customer's quality manager.

## Section 4 – Payment
Payment term is 30 days from invoice date.

## Section 5 – Retention of title
Delivered parts remain the property of the Supplier until full payment.
